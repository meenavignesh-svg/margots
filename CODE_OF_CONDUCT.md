# Code of Conduct

MARGOTS is intended to be a respectful, research-oriented open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Provide reproducible technical or scientific reasoning when reporting problems.
- Respect privacy, licenses, and confidential biological data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, malicious disclosure of secrets, or intentional abuse of project infrastructure are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
